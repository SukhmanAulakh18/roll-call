Roll Call

Watches a bank's AI agents and can stop one that goes wrong, with proof.

Four questions

  1. 1
    What agents exist?
    Every agent has an owner, purpose, risk rating and scopes. Unregistered workloads are refused.
  2. 2
    What can each one touch?
    Tools and scopes are checked on every call against its own allow-list.
  3. 3
    What does each one cost?
    Every call is recorded and metered against a budget. Prices in the demo are assumptions; value is not measured.
  4. 4
    Can I stop one right now, and prove it's stopped?
    A kill revokes its identity, then an outside probe must be refused.
