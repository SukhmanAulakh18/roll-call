# Failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA)

Qualitative. Severity (S), likelihood (L) and detectability are rated High, Medium or Low. No failure rates are given. Each row names the make target that exercises the failure. Where the target is planned rather than present, it is marked "planned". Measured results come from `impact.json` after the target is run; they are not written here.

Scale used:
- Severity: High = agents stopped or uncontrolled spend or unattributed action; Medium = degraded control or unclear evidence; Low = cosmetic or recoverable without effect on governed actions.
- Likelihood: qualitative judgement, given for the laptop demo as built today.
- Detection: how the fault becomes visible to an operator.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Control plane down | No new admissions, identities, kills or incident updates. ## Notes
- Severity High with likelihood Medium (rows 3, 7) are the first items to address in the detector and policy review.
- Rows marked "planned" have no test yet. They must not be cited as tested until the target exists and has been run.
- The FMEA is reviewed after every drill run and after every postmortem.
